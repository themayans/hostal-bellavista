The hostal

Seventy-five years facing the sea

A family-run house on Formentera's only port, a few metres from the quay where the Ibiza ferries dock.

Hostal Bellavista stands on the first line of the marina at La Savina, inside the village and directly opposite the moorings. It is the first building many visitors see when they reach the island — and for a good number of them, the last thing they remember before boarding again.

We have been working for you since 1949, improving the house year after year to give every guest a genuinely personal welcome. Three generations on, we still run the reception and the dining room ourselves.

The hostal has its own bar and restaurant, a large terrace overlooking the port, and car, motorbike, scooter and bicycle hire so you can explore the island at your own pace.

Away from the hostal

Apartments on the island

Alongside the hostal we manage three self-contained apartments around Formentera, each with its own kitchen, for guests who want more space or a longer stay.

Please note: these apartments are NOT in the hostal building. They are separate, self-catering properties elsewhere on the island, with a kitchen and without restaurant or reception service. You will need a vehicle to get around.

Restaurant

Mediterranean cooking and seafood

Welcoming and family-run, in the same building as the hostal, with a large terrace overlooking the port.

You can enjoy all kinds of dishes here, as well as our specialities: lobster and crab stew, grilled fish platters and fish zarzuela, seafood and meat paellas, and fisherman's-style rice.

Among the island dishes we serve you will find fish, mixed and seafood paella and our own paella Bellavista, arroz a la marinera, arroz a banda, fideuà, fresh island fish baked or salt-crusted, and a selection of grilled meats. A broad menu for tasting the cooking of Formentera, with the port in front of you and Ibiza on the horizon.

The surroundings

Formentera, starting from La Savina

Our door opens onto the island's only port, so everything starts here. These are the places we send our guests.

Formentera is more than the last paradise in the Mediterranean to spend a summer holiday in: it is a remarkably well-preserved, quiet island you can drive from end to end in under half an hour.

The mild climate — around 16°C on average between November and April — means you can swim for much of the year, and in autumn and spring the island is at its best: fewer people, cleaner light and softer colours than in high August. A sunset, or simply a look out to sea, justifies the trip on its own.
